01 — About

Computer Vision & ML Engineer with an M.Eng in Engineering Geomatics from the University of Calgary, and peer-reviewed work in navigation, visual odometry and UAV systems.

My work sits where aerial robotics, embedded hardware and real-time perception meet — drones that see, reason and navigate on their own, from GNSS-denied indoor spaces to wide-area methane detection.

At Draganfly I'm leading a Raspberry Pi → NVIDIA Jetson migration and building obstacle avoidance on ROS 2 and PX4. In parallel, I consult for Aerometrix on UAV-based methane detection.
